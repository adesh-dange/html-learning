About MCA Tech Fest 2026

MCA Tech Fest 2026 is a college-level technology event designed for students who are passionate about programming, software development, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

The event provides students with an opportunity to showcase their technical skills, participate in competitions and interact with technology professionals.
